# Brand identity

## MAHFOUZ

MAHFOUZ is a premium online coaching identity for committed clients and serious transformation prospects. It speaks equally to Arabic- and English-speaking audiences in Egypt and internationally. The system should make individual coaching feel structured, credible, personal and achievable.

## Personality order

**Premium → Performance → Discipline → Clarity → Energy**

The brand feels modern, scientific and credible; strong and masculine without aggression; minimal without sterility; motivational without hype; professional without losing approachability. It is confident, not flashy.

## Design principles

1. **Evidence before spectacle.** Real coaching photography and verifiable facts carry the story.
2. **Structure serves the person.** Clear hierarchy and systems make personal coaching legible.
3. **Precision in the details.** Restrained color, spacing, motion and geometry build confidence.
4. **Energy with restraint.** Orange marks action; green denotes progress or functional success.
5. **Language is native.** Arabic is intentionally composed in RTL with Cairo; English uses Manrope and Barlow Condensed display.

## Avoid

Cheap or generic fitness tropes, bodybuilding-gym clichés, playful decoration, extreme futurism, excessive luxury cues, corporate stock styling, visual noise, fake scientific graphics, aggressive language, neon overload and trend-driven novelty.
