# Video style

- **Intro:** establish coach/subject or useful idea immediately; one compact wordmark at most; no complex animated logo or long sting.
- **Lower thirds:** Barlow Condensed name/title hierarchy with Manrope/Cairo supporting line; navy/black field, fine orange cue, restrained geometry.
- **Captions:** native script and direction, short readable lines, safe-area placement, high contrast. Orange may emphasize one meaningful phrase; green only for progress/success meaning. Avoid decorative kinetic word-by-word motion.
- **B-roll:** authentic coaching, training, planning and everyday context; natural light, realistic bodies, no stock clichés presented as the coach/client.
- **Transitions:** direct cuts and subtle fades. Avoid whip, glitch, spin, impact flashes and repetitive zooms.
- **End screen:** one clear next step, wordmark, quiet orange action treatment and enough dwell time to read.
- Respect platform safe areas, reduced-motion where supported, truthful claims, image privacy and source transformation integrity.
