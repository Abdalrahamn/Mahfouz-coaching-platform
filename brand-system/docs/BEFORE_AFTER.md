# Before / after transformations

**Image pixels remain unchanged across all channels.** No body edits, retouching, filters, color shifts, artificial sharpening, face distortion, added typography over the photographs or crop changes that exaggerate the result.

A separate composition may add a simple external frame, divider, or restrained labels when useful. Keep labels outside the source image area. Use neutral type and only approved brand colors. Never invent names, age, duration, weight, measurements, claims, quotes or results. Never reposition the two source images to imply a false timeline or comparison. Watermarks/logos are not placed over source imagery.

Website-specific rule: supplied comparison assets appear as-is, with no overlay, filter, badge or image caption. Preserve originals privately; publish only approved derivatives and permanent privacy sanitization.
