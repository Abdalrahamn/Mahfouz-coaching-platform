# Brand checklist

Before delivery:

- [ ] Palette matches canonical tokens; no decorative hue was introduced.
- [ ] Orange remains the action color; green appears only for functional/progress meaning.
- [ ] Typography roles and Arabic/English direction are correct.
- [ ] Light and dark modes retain identity and readable contrast.
- [ ] Layout uses restrained geometry and spacing; focus and touch targets work.
- [ ] Motion is purposeful and reduced-motion behavior is honored.
- [ ] Images are authentic, privacy-safe and not materially misrepresented.
- [ ] Transformation source pixels remain unchanged; no invented proof or labels.
- [ ] Mobile, wide, Arabic, English and zoomed text layouts remain legible.
- [ ] Any unresolved owner decision is called out rather than guessed.
