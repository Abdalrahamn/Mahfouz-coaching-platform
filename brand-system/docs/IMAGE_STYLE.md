# Image style

## Photography direction

Realistic editorial gym photography. Use directional but natural-looking light, controlled deep contrast, accurate skin tones, neutral/cool surroundings and practical gym environments. A restrained orange practical light may appear when it belongs to the photographed scene. Favor grounded posture, purposeful framing, clean backgrounds and negative space for independent design elements.

## Integrity

Use actual supplied people and approved media. Do not reshape bodies, invent anatomy, distort faces, materially change skin tone, add fake text, logos, badges or data, or fabricate interface screenshots. Do not imply that app illustration is an actual product capture. Do not enhance beyond source detail. Keep client imagery private and sanitized before publication; CSS masking is never privacy protection.

## Image effects

Color grade only new or owner-approved non-proof imagery; keep it restrained and consistent. Grain is optional and barely visible. Glows and overlays should be rare, plausible and outside transformation image pixels. Cropping must preserve truthful context and body proportions. Choose framing for the target composition without excluding important body or identity context.
