# Do and don't

## Do

- Follow the token source and semantic roles.
- Keep premium, performance, discipline, clarity, energy in that order.
- Use realistic editorial photography and honest proof.
- Give Arabic and English native layout and typography.
- Keep dark and light themes intentional and readable.
- Use mostly square geometry, thin borders, quiet shadows and selective motion.
- Ask when a material decision is unresolved.

## Don't

- Add decorative colors because a composition feels empty.
- Make green a co-equal action color.
- Use bodybuilder clichés, neon, fake science, gaming HUDs or generic SaaS cards.
- Retouch, reshape or misrepresent transformation subjects.
- Invent testimonials, credentials, client identity, price claims or results.
- Publish originals, contact sheets or unredacted conversations.
- use motion, effects or novelty that harms clarity, performance or reduced-motion preferences.
