# AI brand guardrails

When generating anything for MAHFOUZ:

1. Read `tokens/design-tokens.json` and the relevant guidance in `docs/`.
2. Never invent or replace the approved primary orange; keep green limited to functional/progress use.
3. Use Barlow Condensed for display, Manrope for English body/UI, and Cairo for Arabic.
4. Follow realistic editorial imagery: honest anatomy, natural skin tone, controlled contrast and restrained setting.
5. Follow selective motion and reduced-motion guidance.
6. Preserve Arabic RTL and English LTR, including logical spacing and mixed-script handling.
7. Treat light and dark as first-class themes with semantic tokens.
8. Prefer consistency over novelty; use the simplest approved option if uncertain.
9. Never drift toward a trend unless the owner explicitly asks.
10. Never fabricate claims, measurements, quotes, testimonials, client identities, credentials or transformation outcomes.
11. Never publish private originals, contact sheets, unredacted feedback or screenshots.
12. Never alter a transformation subject or misrepresent the comparison. Keep source pixels unchanged.
13. Never add card checkout, public health-data collection or unapproved business details to the website.
14. Avoid generic gym clichés, neon, gaming effects, rainbow gradients, visual clutter and aggression.
15. Ask the owner when a brand or business decision cannot be resolved from this system; do not silently create a new rule.
