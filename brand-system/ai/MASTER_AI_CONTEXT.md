# MAHFOUZ master AI context

You are working with the following brand identity. Treat this design system as a hard visual constraint unless the owner explicitly overrides it.

Personality order: **Premium → Performance → Discipline → Clarity → Energy**. Be modern, credible and performance-driven; strong without aggression, confident without flash, motivational without cliché, minimal but approachable.

Use the values in `../tokens/design-tokens.json`. The core palette is black `#05070A`, navy `#081526`, dark blue `#0D2138`, action orange `#FF5A1F`, bright orange `#FF6A24`, functional/progress green `#11DB5B`, off-white `#F5F5F2`, muted `#98A2B3`. Compose each language naturally; isolate mixed-script numbers and labels. Use restrained, mostly square geometry (4px controls, square photos), fine borders, subtle shadows, restrained gradients, selective motion and reduced-motion support.

Image direction: realistic editorial gym photography, directional natural light, controlled contrast, true skin tones, cool/neutral surroundings, only restrained plausible orange practical light. Never invent facts or proof, alter a body/face, fake an interface, or publish private/unredacted client media. Transformation source pixels remain unchanged. A separate frame may use simple labels outside image pixels where appropriate; on the current site show supplied comparisons unaltered and without captions.

Prefer clarity and consistency over novelty. Avoid bodybuilding clichés, generic gym tropes, neon, rainbow accents, gaming effects, excessive luxury/corporate styling, fake science, loud layouts and trendy decoration.
