# MAHFOUZ Reels Brand Brief

## Purpose

Use this file as the single brand reference for creating MAHFOUZ Reels, Stories, TikToks and Shorts. Preserve the same identity across Arabic and English content while re-composing every video for a vertical social canvas.

## Brand identity

**Brand:** MAHFOUZ  
**Business:** Premium online fitness coaching  
**Audience:** Serious coaching clients and transformation prospects in Egypt and internationally.

### Personality priority

1. Premium
2. Performance
3. Discipline
4. Clarity
5. Energy

The tone is confident and credible, strong without aggression, motivating without hype, modern and scientific without fake science, and approachable without losing professionalism.

### Core principles

- Evidence before spectacle: use real coaching, real photography and owner-approved facts.
- Keep the coach and the useful idea central; design should clarify rather than compete.
- Use precise hierarchy, consistent components and controlled contrast.
- Energy comes from pacing and purposeful orange accents, not visual noise.
- Arabic and English are first-class languages with native composition.

### Avoid

Generic bodybuilding clichés, aggressive language, neon overload, excessive luxury cues, playful decoration, fake scientific graphics, stock posing presented as the coach or client, trend-driven novelty, unsupported claims, fake urgency and fabricated results.

## Visual system

### Colors

- Black: `#05070A`
- Deep navy: `#081526`
- Dark blue: `#0D2138`
- Primary action orange: `#FF5A1F`
- Bright orange hover/highlight: `#FF6A24`
- Off-white: `#F5F5F2`
- Muted text: `#98A2B3`
- Performance green: `#11DB5B` — use only for truthful progress, success or included states.

Use black/navy as the foundation. Orange is for CTA, a small emphasis cue or active state. Do not introduce decorative accent hues. Do not flood the frame with orange.

### Typography

- Display/headlines/wordmark: **Barlow Condensed**, weight 600–800.
- English body and UI: **Manrope**, weight 400–800.
- Arabic body, UI and captions: **Cairo**, weight 400–800.
- Do not artificially condense Arabic or apply Latin-style tracking to Arabic.
- Keep text large enough for mobile viewing and use high contrast.

### Shape and layout

- Quiet grid, generous negative space and restrained square geometry.
- Prefer square or 4px corners; use rounded shapes only when they serve a compact utility control.
- Use hairline borders and soft shadows sparingly.
- Re-compose for each aspect ratio; never stretch one layout across every platform.

## Reel format and composition

- Default canvas: vertical 9:16.
- Keep faces, essential copy and CTA clear of platform interface chrome.
- Maintain generous top and bottom safe areas.
- One main idea per Reel.
- Start with the coach, a useful visual or the core message immediately.
- Use a quiet MAHFOUZ wordmark at most once; never use a long logo sting.
- End with one clear next step, a small wordmark and enough dwell time to read.

## Video direction

### Openings

Open directly with a useful visual, statement, coaching action or problem. Avoid introductions that delay the value.

### B-roll

Use authentic training, planning, nutrition, coaching-app and everyday context. Prefer realistic bodies, natural directional light, true skin tones and cool/neutral surroundings. Never invent anatomy, alter bodies or present generic stock footage as a client result.

### Editing and motion

- Prefer direct cuts and subtle fades.
- Motion should be short, purposeful and readable.
- Use restrained reveals, image masks and micro-interactions only when they improve understanding.
- Avoid glitch, spin, whip transitions, impact flashes, repetitive zooms, bounce, excessive parallax and scroll-like motion.
- Honor reduced-motion preferences where the platform supports them.

### Lower thirds

Use Barlow Condensed for the name/title hierarchy and Manrope or Cairo for the supporting line. Keep the field black/navy, add one fine orange cue and use restrained geometry.

### Captions

- Use native script and direction: Arabic RTL, English LTR.
- Keep lines short, high contrast and inside safe areas.
- Emphasize one meaningful phrase with orange only when needed.
- Green is reserved for actual progress or success meaning.
- Avoid decorative word-by-word kinetic captions.
- Captions must remain readable without sound.

### End card

Use one owner-approved CTA only. Keep it quiet and direct, with the MAHFOUZ wordmark, an orange action treatment and enough reading time. Do not add discounts, scarcity, fake urgency or unsupported promises.

## Writing style

Write in a clear, disciplined and direct voice.

Use:

- Short sentences and concrete coaching language.
- Credible explanations instead of hype.
- Practical actions the viewer can understand immediately.
- A confident but respectful tone.
- Native Arabic phrasing for Arabic versions, not literal word-for-word translation.

Avoid:

- Clichés such as “no pain, no gain.”
- Shaming bodies, medical claims or guaranteed outcomes.
- Invented numbers, testimonials, ratings, client names or transformation timelines.
- Overpromising speed, certainty or universal results.

## Privacy and evidence rules

- Use only supplied, approved and privacy-safe client media.
- Transformation source photos remain unchanged in their original private library.
- Never publish `private/`, originals, contact sheets or unredacted screenshots.
- Never add overlays, filters, badges or captions directly onto supplied transformation images unless the approved medium-specific composition requires it.
- Do not identify clients or expose names, phone numbers, usernames, health details or private conversation context.
- MP4 files are not automatically testimonials.
- The recognizable person in branded coach photography is the real coach.

## Recommended Reel structure

1. **Hook:** useful statement or visual in the first moment.
2. **Proof/context:** authentic coach footage, training action or clear explanation.
3. **Teaching:** one practical point, not a list of unrelated tips.
4. **Payoff:** the decision or action the viewer should understand.
5. **CTA:** one clear owner-approved next step.
